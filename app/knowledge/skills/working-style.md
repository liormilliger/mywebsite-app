# How Lior works

**Summary:** An owner who sees the big picture, learns fast, and is the person colleagues call when
they're stuck.

## Evidence
- **Ownership:** sees systems through from design to production and keeps them running; after a
  production incident he built the recovery story himself rather than only fixing the symptom.
- **Proactive, with ideas and fast delivery:** managers have repeatedly told him they value his
  proactivity, his ideas and his delivery times.
- **Big-picture thinking:** designs whole systems (edge and cloud halves, GitOps state vs charts,
  teardown and recovery), not just the piece in front of him.
- **Fast learner:** productive with a new tool within hours; deeper, complex tools within a few
  days. Went from 30 years as a professional musician to bootcamps, three certifications (AWS CCP,
  AWS SAA, CKA) and production platform and AI engineering within about three years.
- **Go-to person and mentor:** a focal point for advice among DevOps peers and people he has met
  along the way. Example: helped a friend stuck in a database migration by building a PostgreSQL
  view that aggregated data from several tables, in a couple of minutes.
- **Team player:** works in shared repositories with other engineers through branches, pull
  requests and review fixes; writes documentation, runbooks and changelogs so others can operate
  what he builds.
- **Leadership and discipline from music:** founded and conducted a 20-piece big band and built
  music-education networks for whole municipalities, leading people and logistics toward one goal.
- **Calm under pressure, service mindset:** supported enterprise customers' production pipelines
  at JFrog.
