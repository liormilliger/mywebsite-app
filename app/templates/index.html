About Me

From music to infrastructure — a career built on orchestrating complex systems

Experienced DevOps and Infrastructure Engineer, taking end-to-end ownership of production environments, from bare-metal and edge devices to highly available Kubernetes clusters.

Specialized in Kubernetes architecture, Helm chart design, and CI/CD pipelines (Jenkins, GitHub Actions, Bitbucket Pipelines) with GitOps automation (FluxCD, ArgoCD). Hands-on with Ansible for zero-touch provisioning and k3s for air-gapped edge deployments, including full monolith-to-Kubernetes migrations for production environments.

Designing multi-region AWS platforms (CloudFormation, SAM) with VPN-first architectures (WireGuard) linking edge deployments to cloud-hosted services. Layered IaC with Terraform, Terragrunt, and CloudFormation for modular, reusable infrastructure.

Building agentic AI systems and organizational knowledge infrastructure, including an Organizational Brain reasoning layer, an AI-awareness layer, and multi-session Claude architectures. Working with LangChain, embedding models, and Qdrant to power retrieval and memory for these pipelines.

What drives me is ownership — seeing a system through from design to production, and being the person a team can rely on when something breaks. I pick up new tools and domains fast, thrive on ambiguity, and believe the best infrastructure gets built by teams that trust each other, not by any one person working alone.
